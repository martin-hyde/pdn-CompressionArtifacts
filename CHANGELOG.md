# Changelog

## v1.0.0

First public release.

- Preview and apply compression artifacts inside paint.net, with its normal preview, cancellation, and undo workflow.
- JPEG, JPEG XL, JPEG XR, WebP, and AVIF codec round trips, plus an explicitly labelled HEIC approximation.
- Codec-specific controls, JPEG background colors and optional alpha preservation, and multithreaded AVIF encoding.
- Supports paint.net 5.2 or newer on Windows x64.
